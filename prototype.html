Why We Exist

We Fell Out of Love With Tourist Buses. So We Built Kiyara.

Too much travel in Sri Lanka has become hurried: checking off sights from a checklist, rushing through crowded temples in huge tour groups, and eating buffet food made for tourists rather than locals.

Kiyara Holidays was born from a simpler longing: to introduce our island home with dignity, depth, and unhurried grace. When you travel with us, you are introduced as an honored guest to our personal friends—our favorite boutique lodge owners, generational tea masters, and veteran safari spotters.
